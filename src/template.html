Villagers choose their own work: put up a workplace and someone will staff it. Everyone eats twice a day, winter freezes the lake and kills crops, and anyone without a home can freeze. If mood stays below 22% for a whole day, your people will leave. Each season lasts 3 days, so a year is 12 days. Happy villagers earn Favor for god powers.
